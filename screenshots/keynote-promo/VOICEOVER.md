# Liftmark — "Same Wall" · launch film voiceover

**Voice:** low, close, confident. Think premium sports-tech launch: short sentences, no hype words,
a beat of air between lines. Never smile through the first three lines; let the warmth in at "New best."

**Film:** 36 s, vertical. Drop each line at its time (±0.3 s is fine). **Bold** words land on a visible hit.
Music: something minimal and pulsing (90–100 bpm), dropping out at 17.6 s and slamming back in at 19.4 s.

## Recording tips

- One take per line, a second of room tone before and after, so they slide onto the times.
- Leave 17.6–19.4 nearly silent: the film holds its breath there on purpose.
- Mix the voice about 8–10 dB over the sound effects; duck the music under it.
