# Liftmark — "The 77.5" · voiceover script

**Voice:** one narrator, dry and warm, talking to the lifter like a training partner who has seen it all.
Unhurried, a little deadpan, never salesy. Smile on "Liftmark did." and on "...ten."

**Film:** 46 s, vertical. Record the lines anywhere and drop each one at its time below
(the picture is built to these times; ±0.3 s is fine). Words in **bold** land on a visible hit:
lean on them a little.

| In | Out | Line | What's on screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.4 | 3.4 | "**Six weeks.** Same bar. Same seventy-seven and a half." | A paper logbook on the table, the number 77.5 cut out of magazine letters |
| 3.8 | 7.0 | "You logged it every single time. Your notebook never said a word." | A typed slip clacks out: 6 weeks · 12 sessions · 77.5 kg |
| 7.6 | 8.6 | "Liftmark **did**." | The real Liftmark card slaps down on the notebook |
| 9.2 | 13.0 | "It reads every set, and it spotted the **stall** before you'd admit it." | A red STALLING stamp hits the card |

Word count: about 105. Pace: around 150 words a minute with the pauses.

## Alternates (same timing)

## Recording tips

- Record each line as its own take, with a breath of silence before and after; that makes them easy to slide onto the times above.
- Leave the silence after "Back at seventy-seven and a half..." empty: the film holds still there on purpose.
- Mix the voice about 10 dB above the paper sounds already in the film.
